# Strategy, narrative and voice

## Choose what is worth publishing

Start from the reader's job: solve a doubt, recognize a problem, compare options, understand a mechanism or make a decision. Define one defensible idea and a concrete reason to consume it now. The offer enters when it helps that decision.

| Goal | Value to deliver | Signal to watch if access exists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant discovery | Recognizable situation, demonstration or accessible explanation | Relevant reach and profile visits |
| Usefulness/consideration | Applicable steps, comparison criteria, checklist | Saves, shares and specific questions |
| Conversation | Answerable question about a real experience | Substantive replies and qualified inquiries |
| Sale | Mechanism, evidence, objection and next step | Contacts and attributable conversions |

Do not default to reach when the goal is buyers. Do not fabricate controversy, urgency, monetary losses or personal experiences. An educational piece does not need to sell on every slide.

For complex services, show a concrete operation: what goes in, what the system does and what the person receives. Separate available capability, illustrative example and possible evolution. A first stage is presented as an offer only if it exists and its scope is confirmed. Prices, timelines and third-party cases are not evidence for the brand.

## Script that holds the promise

- Specific hook, understandable without the caption. The promise is kept in the body
- Each slide adds something new: proof, case, mechanism, step, real contrast or consequence
- Support expands the headline. Remove two wordings of the same sentence
- A tutorial shows the how: a realistic query, a verified command, a walkthrough, a decision or a complete example
- A warning explains the failure mechanism and a useful action. Use an analogy only if it clarifies, never to meet a quota
- Closing tied to the topic: an applicable criterion, a precise question or a concrete CTA. No mandatory aphorism, fake mic drop or generic sign-off
- Complementary caption: context, nuance, source or next step. Length depends on the channel and message. Do not force 1-3 lines
- Alt text when the channel supports it: convey the meaning and main data of the image, without repeating hashtags

Propose up to two distinct hooks when it settles an editorial doubt. Do not produce two complete carousels by default. Slide count depends on value and legibility, within confirmed channel limits.

## Facts and examples

Record each claim: literal text, source, date, scope and status. A statistic needs denominator, unit, period and a correct comparison. A product capability needs a catalog, demo or available confirmation.

`[VERIFICAR: fuente del ahorro]` is a draft marker. Before final, resolve, remove or reword it. A hypothesis does not become fact through aesthetic approval.

Plausible fictional examples can be built to teach, labeled as such. Do not present them as a real interface, testimonial, client case or proof of results. Protect identifiable third-party data.

## Anti-slop review

Apply this anti-slop review:

1. Identify the point and the voice's own signals before editing
2. Remove phrases interchangeable between businesses, empty openings and adjectives that stand in for evidence
3. Check repeated binary contrasts, colon reveals, dramatic fragments, uniform cadence and pseudo-profound closings
4. Keep authentic detail, humor, uncertainty and opinions. Do not homogenize everything
5. Edit the minimum, read the whole and also review chart labels, microcopy and caption

This review detects patterns. It does not determine AI authorship. Words or structures are not defects out of context. Do not adopt an English banned-word list as literal censorship for any language.

Punctuation, emojis, capitalization and tone come from the brand profile. No editorial preference of one project should become a universal rule.

## Interaction, reuse and measurement

A CTA must describe an available action. If it promises a guide by comment, verify real delivery and an owner. If it does not exist yet, change the CTA or prepare the resource within the authorized scope. A poll image is not a working sticker: separate the asset from the native setup step.

Adapt one idea by function: carousel for navigable explanation; story for interaction and replies; reel to demonstrate over time (see the `motion-video-studio` skill); LinkedIn for professional context. Do not change only the size.

To optimize: compare own data from the same period, goal and format, and separate paid from organic. Document the hypothesis, the variable to change, the metric and the evaluation date. A few observations do not prove causality. Verify posting times, frequencies, hashtags, reach and platform policies when the decision depends on them. Do not promise a diagnosed shadowban or universal formulas.
